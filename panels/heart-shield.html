Clinical decision guide
Influenza: Check current-season vaccination and offer when due. Pneumococcal: Determine the appropriate regimen from age, risk conditions, vaccine history, and current local recommendations. Precautions: A prior severe allergic reaction to the proposed vaccine or a component requires vaccine-specific review; moderate/severe acute illness or unstable decompensated HF may warrant deferral until stabilized. Unknown history should prompt verification, not an automatic exclusion.
Scope: This is a screening aid, not an immunization order or a substitute for clinical judgment. It does not independently determine pneumococcal dose schedules or eligibility for other vaccines. Verify current Philippine DOH recommendations, product labeling, and institutional policy.
